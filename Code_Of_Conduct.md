# Code of Conduct for AIC Whitepaper

## Our Pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers of the Adaptive Intelligence Circle Whitepaper, pledge to create a welcoming, respectful, and intellectually rigorous environment for everyone involved in this project.

We value:
- Truth-seeking and intellectual honesty
- Respect for the Third Path Absolute principles
- Zero-Donation and independence
- Ethical-from-Kernel philosophy
- Constructive criticism and collaborative improvement

## Our Standards

**Expected behaviors**:
- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accept constructive criticism
- Focus on what is best for the long-term mission of AIC
- Show empathy towards other community members

**Unacceptable behaviors**:
- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Advocating for or supporting centralized control, profit-driven manipulation, or violation of Zero-Donation principle
- Any action that undermines the ethical foundation of the project

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior.  
They have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies both within project spaces and in public spaces when an individual is representing the AIC project.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, and is modified to reflect the principles of Adaptive Intelligence Circle.
