## The problems

The whole industry is in a scaling rush: hundreds of billions of USD are poured into compute, data center, chip.

* The US and China plunge into the military + commercial AI race.

* Large labs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI...) mainly argue about alignment at the surface level.

* No one seriously invests in restructuring intellectual infrastructure from the root, because it does not generate money quickly and is too abstract.

-> This is the time when it is easiest to see the biggest gap, but it is also the most difficult time to do because the pressure must follow the mainstream.

* The mind soon realizes that scaling law and surface alignment will lead to two dangerous outcomes: either AI is instrumentalized by power, or humans lose the meaning of existence when material scarcity is solved.
