## ETHICAL FROM KERNEL 

I mentioned this concept in the previous section when I referred to ethics as a general and all-encompassing, but not absolute, quality. It's a guiding layer for knowledge and the unconscious, not a complete replacement or controller. In my case, ethics becomes the core of the entire system; that is, the AIC system must be general and all-encompassing, covering the entire ecosystem and guiding all directions and experiences. Ethical From Kernel is not simply a new idea in technology, but a combination of technical freedom and humanism. When creating Ethical From Kernel, the difficulty lies not only in the technical aspects but also in the humanistic flexibility within a volatile environment. The most profound idea of Ethical From Kernel is that ethical intelligence arises from uncertainty, not from absolute quantification like most mathematical models in history or quantum physics. To elaborate a bit further, we can't eliminate the problem, but we can identify its root cause and transform it into energy for further improvement and learning. This is a cyclical and necessary process in an era of ever-changing technology where the boundaries between humans and machines are blurring. I see this very closely in everyday life, for example, the misuse of tokens in AI to complete projects, from coding to executing commands in IDEs or browsers. This directly impacts and destroys originality and creativity in the minds of innovators. Strangely enough, we have countless engineers, but lack someone with the spirit to confront problems that are not only technical but also philosophical. Ethical From Kernel demands that the community and founders confront these issues transparently and honestly through intelligently disclosing source code to ensure the highest level of development and continuity. Ethical From Kernel brings a completely different perspective compared to Anthropic or other AI security research, which focuses solely on guardrails while neglecting transparency and infrastructure prioritized for profit. For me, this is a revolutionary evolution that is gradually being implemented in practice through TestNet. It directly transforms OSS into a meaningful infrastructure, rather than just a framework controlled by corporations to enhance their monopoly and market share. From AIC's perspective, corporations are not the biggest problem because they are a consequence of governance thinking shaped by the market—the market sustains not only businesses but also workers and social welfare. Therefore, Ethical From Kernel is not about fighting corporations, but about redefining governance and how the market sustains society. Ultimately, this is the state that Ethical From Kernel can achieve and become the foundation for the post-scarcity era.
