# Zero-donation protocol (orientation)

AIC’s design target is that **protocol rule-power is not for sale**.

- Operational costs and optional support channels (if any) must not purchase votes over kernel principles.  
- “Zero donation” in the strong sense means refusing funding models that convert capital into governance capture.  
- Host fees, fiscal sponsorship mechanics, and personal income of contributors remain subject to **tax and law**; they are not a moral free pass and not a substitute for Charter constraints.

Details of any fundraising surface belong in governance announcements — never as silent whitepaper amendments.
